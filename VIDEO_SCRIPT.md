## 📝 RECORDING TIPS:

1. **Screen Record:**
   - Use QuickTime (Mac) or OBS
   - Record at 1920x1080 (Full HD)
   - 30 FPS minimum

2. **Audio:**
   - Use headphones with mic
   - Quiet room
   - Clear, energetic voice
   - Smile while talking (it shows!)

3. **Pacing:**
   - Practice once before recording
   - Keep it tight - no rambling
   - Hit the 2-minute mark exactly

4. **Visuals:**
   - Move mouse slowly & deliberately
   - Pause briefly on each feature
   - Show, don't just tell

5. **Energy:**
   - Be excited! You're solving a real problem
   - Speak confidently
   - End with impact
